How it works

A voice beside you, not a plaque behind glass

The stories are written for walking. Each place holds many spots — a sanctum, a mandapa, a viewpoint — and each one has its own short guide, in more than one language.

  1. 1

    Choose a place

    Begin somewhere like Hampi, the ruined capital spread across river, palms and stone.

    Virupaksha Temple, the Vittala stone chariot, a ridge at sunset — open the guide when you arrive.

  3. 3

    Listen in your language

    English, Hindi, Kannada and more, read in a voice chosen for the place.

Hampi

Where the first stories live

Hampi is our first home. The Virupaksha gopuram still watches over a living temple. A few minutes away, the stone chariot stands in the Vittala courtyard, wheels carved so finely they almost look ready to move. Between them, boulder hills and banana groves hold the rest of Vijayanagara.

Put your earphones in at the gate, and let the walk set the pace. This is not a lecture. It is a companion for the path you are already on.
